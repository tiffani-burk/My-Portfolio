AI‑Powered Job Description Consolidation Tool

Streamlining the job description consolidation process for HR job analysts through automation and AI‑assisted workflows.

Business Ask

Create an AI‑powered application to streamline the job description consolidation process for a specialized HR Transformation team.

  • Users: Team of specialized HR job analysts
  • Role: UX Designer & Researcher
  • Timeline: 6 weeks
  • Tools: Figma, Miro, Notion

The Problem

The team of job analysts faces significant time and efficiency challenges due to the manual process of consolidating job descriptions.

  • Time‑Consuming: Several days required to consolidate a single job description.
  • Inefficient Resource Allocation: Analysts cannot dedicate time to other critical tasks.
  • Error‑Prone: Manual side‑by‑side comparison leads to inconsistencies and human error.

User Research

To understand the workflow and pain points, I conducted research across three areas: user flows, competitive analysis, and usability testing.

User Flow

The user flow outlines how analysts move from uploading job descriptions to generating a consolidated version.

Competitive Analysis

I evaluated existing tools to identify gaps and opportunities for differentiation.

User Testing

I conducted 5 moderated usability tests with job analysts. Each participant completed scenario‑based tasks while I observed their interactions and gathered feedback.

  • Identify usability issues within the design
  • Gather quantitative and qualitative feedback
  • Uncover key insights to guide iteration
